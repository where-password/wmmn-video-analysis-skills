# Related Skills

The general video analysis skill is intentionally the entry point of a small ecosystem.

## Professional Video Analysis

Status: planned.

For comics, animation, teaching, product demonstrations, and other work with a measurable production structure. It focuses on feasibility, shot breakdown, camera/editing structure, reusable production knowledge, and prompt-ready evidence.

It does not claim to explain humor, net sense, personality, audience response, or guaranteed virality.

## Long Video Index

Status: planned.

For tens or hundreds of hours of video. It prioritizes subtitles, chapters, topic maps, hierarchical summaries, timestamps, and local retrieval. It locates candidate segments and hands selected ranges to the professional analysis skill; it does not perform full professional critique by itself.

The host agent owns routing between these skills. This package does not auto-install or silently invoke them.
