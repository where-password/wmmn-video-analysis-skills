# Related Skills

The general video analysis Skill is an independent entry point. It does not require another Skill to produce a basic content overview.

## Professional Video Analysis

Status: available as \`professional-video-analysis\`.

For users who want to study or reverse-engineer production methods, shots, pacing, camera, transitions, or prompts, especially for comic, animation, and AIGC-style narrative work. It works within its own explicit evidence and observation boundaries.

## Long Video Index

Status: in development as \`long-video-index\`.

For transcript-backed long content such as courses, lectures, interviews, and streams. It builds a timestamp index, supports locating quotes or sections, and keeps an evidence trail. It is a separate product direction from professional comic/AIGC analysis.

## Routing

The general Skill may suggest one or both directions when the content and user intent justify it. Suggestions are optional. The Skill does not install, invoke, or orchestrate another Skill automatically.
